## Aggregate signals

- **Verified path completed 8/10 cases**; cases 1 and 10 were marked `unverified` after a Groq `json_validate_failed` 400. The harness ran to completion either way — no case crashed the run.
- **Verified-Redress confirmed the correct regulator in 5/10 cases** outright (3, 5, 7, 8, 9 — with 9 also being a baseline-fabrication catch).
- **3 verified-Redress picks were real but wrong** (2 — PHSO ombudsman for telecom, 4 — NCC for housing, 6 — Colorado UCA for Australia utility). Tavily returned plausible-but-off-topic pages; the model didn't reject them. This is the dominant residual failure mode.
- **Baseline hallucinated outright in 1/10 cases** (case 9 — `housingstate.gov` for Singapore, case 10 — HMRC for NZ). Baseline also produced unverifiable contact emails in several cases (e.g. `info@lagos.gov.ng`, `complaints@cmc.org.za`).

## Inter-case delay

- Increased from ~4s → **25s** to stay under Groq's 8000 TPM limit. With each case using ~2000–3500 tokens across baseline + verified calls, the 25s gap keeps us safely under the cap.
- Total runtime for the full 10-case run: ~5 minutes.
